The LiDAR shows what the record describes: a red ring (the outer bank) with a blue ring immediately inside it (the interior ditch), exactly as the record states. The record describes the form as a circular enclosure with walls surrounding an interior ditch; the measured bank heights and ditch depths are what the 1 m LiDAR reads from the ground surface today.

The Great Circle Earthworks spans 362 m measured (about 366 m documented), with banks rising up to 2.6 m and an interior ditch dropping 2.7 m below the baseline, and it is already on the UNESCO World Heritage list as part of Hopewell Ceremonial Earthworks (2023).
