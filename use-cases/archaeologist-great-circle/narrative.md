**Form, as the record describes it:** a circular enclosure whose walls surround an interior ditch, with a gateway nearly 200 ft wide and Eagle Mound at the center. The LiDAR shows this directly: a red ring (the outer bank, raised above its surroundings) with a blue ring immediately inside it (the ditch, cut below grade), running continuously around the interior. The transect is a verified east-west line through the site's centre.
**Culture and period:** Hopewell, between 1 and 400 CE.
The Great Circle Earthworks spans 362 m measured (about 366 m documented), a Hopewell circular enclosure with an interior ditch and walls rising up to 2.6 m above the baseline, already on the UNESCO World Heritage list as part of Hopewell Ceremonial Earthworks (2023).
