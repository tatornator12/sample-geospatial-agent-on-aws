Two of the three sites like the Octagon Earthworks still show in the ground; one is faint.

- **Hopeton Earthworks**, 87 km: still shows, circle and square enclosure traced as continuous ploughed walls.
- **High Bank Works**, 93 km: still shows, circle and octagon walls both traceable across agricultural fields.
- **Seip Earthworks**, 112 km: faint, enclosure walls reduced to low, broad rises only.

At High Bank Works the LiDAR still traces the same circle-and-octagon form as the Octagon Earthworks. They are already on the World Heritage list.
